Opening

Throwing 26 objects

Reconstruction

1. The staircase problem

Standard temporal compression, as in common video VAEs, loses high-frequency information and produces “staircase” artifacts: unnatural, abrupt temporal deformations. Directly compressing world coordinates entangles features across scales and fails to preserve geometric detail. Our holistic ST-VAE, which decouples rigid motion from deformation, removes the staircase problem and keeps the geometric detail.

2. Held-out & OOD

Ground truth (left) and ST-VAE reconstruction (right) on held-out Objaverse scenes, followed by out-of-distribution (OOD) objects from GSO and Toys4K. The ST-VAE reconstructs the OOD objects zero-shot. Achieves ~2.48 mm precision on meter-scale scenes. The Young’s moduli were chosen to show both soft bodies with large deformation and stiff, nearly rigid ones, so some values do not match the real material of the object.

Prediction

8. Chaos

Five reruns of the same scene with identical initial input on a GPU IPC solver end in significantly different states. In this regime deterministic regression tends to produce damped, non-physical averages, whereas diffusion better models their distribution.

Differentiable Applications

14. Task 1: Material estimation

Given a single trajectory of a body with unknown stiffness, PhysLDM recovers its Young’s modulus by gradient descent through the model, within 0.06 dex in about 40 seconds on all seven test bodies. DiffIPC solves the task only after hand-tuning its solver and then needs 26 minutes on 48 threads; Newton/Warp fails in the backward pass.

15. Task 2: Designing the throw

Which throw reveals the stiffness best? We maximize the sensitivity of the predicted trajectory to the stiffness over the release velocity. The objective is itself a derivative, so its gradient is second-order; PhysLDM provides it directly through automatic differentiation. Replayed in the ground-truth simulator, the optimized throw makes the rigid motion 13× more sensitive to the stiffness. DiffIPC offers only first-order derivatives, and Newton/Warp does not produce a usable gradient on this scene.

Constitutive-Model-Agnostic Supervision

16. ARAP

Ground truth (left) and zero-shot ST-VAE reconstruction (right) under an as-rigid-as-possible (ARAP) energy. The ST-VAE was trained only on Stable Neo-Hookean simulations, so this energy model never appears in training.

17. Real-world data

Seamless support for other data formats, such as surface meshes and point clouds. Our training data are tetrahedral meshes, but PhysLDM runs on these formats without any change to the model or the training code. Shown here are real captured point clouds from PhysTwin: tracked points (left) and zero-shot ST-VAE reconstruction (right). There is no mesh and no explicit energy model, and the objects are manipulated by a human hand instead of being thrown onto the ground.
